Aim for two pages. Delete the guidance lines.

## Summary

Three sentences: the problem, the proposed decision, the main cost.

## Problem

What happens today, who is affected, why now. List fixed constraints and non-goals. No solutions yet.

## Criteria

What a good answer must and should do. Agree these before comparing options.

## Options

Include "do nothing". For each: what it is, strengths, weaknesses. Link evidence for factual claims and label assumptions.

## Decision

What we will do, why, what we give up, and where the decision stops applying.

## Dissent

Unresolved objections, in terms their authors accept. Write "None" if none.

## Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |

## MVP scope

The smallest delivery that tests the decision. State what is in and out.

## Success signals and revisit trigger

Observable signals, when they will be checked, and what result reopens the decision.

## Open questions

Each with an owner. Say which must be answered before acceptance.
